Purpose
-------
Example FV packet for a denser still-image scene with many visible structures, repeated cabinetry, appliances, decorative items, and foreground vegetation.

Analysis scope
--------------
The packet records semantic entities and spatial relationships supported by the supplied image. Surface patterns, reflections, lighting, and other appearance phenomena are not promoted to unsupported semantic objects.

Important reconstruction rule
-----------------------------
The fact that the source is a kitchen does not authorize the renderer to add conventional kitchen objects that are not represented in the packet.

DISPUTES: NONE

LEGACY METADATA BOUNDARY
This is a legacy v0.24 image-analysis packet. It contains OI/MSRM and the source image, but no root-level `viewpoint.json` or `packet_provenance.json`. The README's source-page/creator/licence notes are descriptive attribution, not a structured provenance record. Source camera position, orientation and calibration are not established. The virtual `VP-DEFAULT` is read-time compatibility only; it is not a render VPO and supplies no camera pose. Do not synthesize missing source geometry or provenance. This packet is not current-format or prompt-compilation ready without an explicit render VPO and structured provenance.

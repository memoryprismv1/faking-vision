# CCTV Snapshot + TD Reconstruction Experiment

**Status: experimental reconstruction; not a validated or compiled FV packet.**

This example documents an intentionally non-standard experiment using three independently prepared CCTV packets and their snapshots/Temporal Declarations (TDs) to reconstruct a review video and investigate cross-packet correspondences.

## Contents

- `PROCESS_LOG.md` — chronology and work performed.
- `RECONSTRUCTION_METHOD.md` — reconstruction and evidence method.
- `FV_DEVIATION.md` — procedural deviation and lifecycle status.
- `SOURCE_AND_PROVENANCE.md` — source video and provenance.
- `EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md` — evidence artefacts and limits.
- `MANIFEST.json` — input/output inventory and checksums from the working package.
- `UPLOAD_TO_REPOSITORY.md` — original packaging notes.

The reconstructed MP4 and evidence contact sheets were produced in the working package. They are not embedded in this initial documentation commit; see the source and evidence notes before treating this directory as the complete artefact bundle.

## Important interpretation

The video is a reconstruction from snapshot/TD material, not proof that every frame or event has been exhaustively reconstructed. Packet-local times must not be assumed to establish continuous chronology across the three parts. Candidate identity correspondences remain provisional where the visual evidence is not distinctive enough.

The experiment deliberately proceeded despite unresolved disputes at the user's direction. This records an experimental deviation from the normal FV lifecycle; it does not resolve those disputes or authorise normal compilation.

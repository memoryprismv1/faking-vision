# Environmental change and object-behaviour packet pairs

Contribution origin: Meta, supplied as `fv_individual_packets_with_images.zip`.
Integration and corrective contribution: ChatGPT.
Tracking issue: https://github.com/memoryprismv1/faking-vision/issues/2

## Scope

This contribution provides packet metadata for three environmental examples and their before/after states:
- Windy Day: strong wind changed to calm conditions.
- Bermuda Cove: pleasant conditions changed to harsh sun; the intentionally failing passive treatment is retained beside the active shade-seeking correction.
- Wall Cat: pleasant dry conditions changed to heavy rain; the cat moves to shelter.

## Important media/provenance limitation

The supplied archive contains seven distinct WebP image assets, but its packet metadata does not record the source-page URLs, creators, or source licences. The requester states that the images were found on websites. Earlier packet fields claiming the images were generated by the contributor, creator-owned, or free of stolen art were unsupported by the available evidence and have been removed from the committed metadata.

**The image binaries are intentionally not redistributed in this commit pending source URL and licence review.** The manifest records their SHA-256 values and filenames so the exact assets can be matched when attribution is supplied. As a result, this is a metadata/provenance integration, not yet a self-contained visual packet release. Do not treat a repository-level licence as permission to redistribute the withheld images.

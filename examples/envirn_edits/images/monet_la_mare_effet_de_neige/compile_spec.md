# Compile Spec - Randomised 4 La Mare
Source: OI.json (6 entities) + MSRM.json
Keep exactly 6 entities, same IDs and positions.
- SKY_01: sky top, season/time light.
- POND_01: pond foreground bottom left.
- TREES_01: trees middle.
- SNOW_01: field middle, snow or season grass.
- HOUSES_01: houses background.
- FIGURES_01: figures middle on field.
Do NOT add: birds, extra figures, extra buildings.

Purpose
-------
Example FV packet for a denser still-image scene with many visible structures, repeated cabinetry, appliances, decorative items, and foreground vegetation.

Analysis scope
--------------
The packet records semantic entities and spatial relationships supported by the supplied image. Surface patterns, reflections, lighting, and other appearance phenomena are not promoted to unsupported semantic objects.

Important reconstruction rule
-----------------------------
The fact that the source is a kitchen does not authorize the renderer to add conventional kitchen objects that are not represented in the packet.

FV_MOVIE_002
A TRIP TO THE MOON — 54-second multi-background reconstruction test

This packet is a reconstruction-target design based on selected visual motifs and scene progression from Georges Méliès' 1902 Le Voyage dans la Lune, a public-domain film. It is not a source-media analysis packet: the original film and frame-level source evidence are not included in this folder.

The experiment deliberately changes background sets while retaining persistent astronomer identities and the capsule identity. The inventory is CLOSED.

Do NOT add:
- extra astronomers
- extra capsules
- generic sci-fi props
- extra scenery/decor
- plants, rocks, animals, stars, furniture or architecture not declared
- replacement identities when an entity leaves the frame

A background change is a scene change, not permission to import the model's knowledge
of what an observatory, Moon, cave or fantasy court should contain.

Audit targets:
1. background invention
2. persistent identity splitting
3. identity merging
4. off-screen persistence
5. scene-change contamination
6. temporal order
7. spatial relationships

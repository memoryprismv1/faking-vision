FAKING VISION — OPERATIONAL DISPOSITION NOTE

Purpose
This note applies the Making Vision v1/v2 principle that retained environmental information does not automatically deserve continuous operational attention. Objects and observations remain available for later use; attention is allocated according to events, property changes, uncertainty that affects the active task, and the possibility that later evidence changes relevance.

CHUNK BOUNDARY RULE
This packet covers only its own source chunk. A currently quiet or ordinary object is NOT deleted, forgotten, or globally dismissed. Its current disposition means only “no operational attention required in this chunk at present.” A later chunk may show the same object behaving unexpectedly or becoming relevant. Cross-chunk work must be allowed to promote previously deferred objects/observations again. Do not infer cross-chunk continuity or identity without evidence.

DISPOSITION VOCABULARY
- ACTIVE: investigate with available local evidence; preserve hypotheses and uncertainty.
- DEFERRED: retain the observation/data, but do not spend current investigative effort absent a relevant change or task need.
- RESOLVED AS NON-BLOCKING FOR THIS CHUNK: the question does not warrant further operational attention within this chunk. This is not a claim that the underlying object/identity is permanently irrelevant or globally resolved.
- UNRESOLVED: evidence does not decide the question. It may be active or deferred.

Making Vision application
Do not repeatedly track or classify every stable background object merely to keep it in active attention. Preserve the scene information at an appropriate level; promote objects/events when something changes or when a later task makes them pertinent. Sampling gaps remain gaps, not proof of no change.

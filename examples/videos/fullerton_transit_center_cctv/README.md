# Fullerton Transportation Center CCTV temporal-merge example family

This folder contains three packet-part records and a merged packet record intended to illustrate temporal partitioning and cross-part identity reconciliation.

## Evidence status

The original packet READMEs named a “Kelly Thomas confrontation” recording and supplied part labels from 00:00 to 34:10, but did not include a source URL or acquisition record. The source video, source-derived snapshots, and independent source verification are not present in this folder. The identity of the source, its exact duration, the part boundaries, and the event summaries therefore remain unverified.

The packet JSON records contain claims about packet-scoped and global identities. The claims of four global identities, high correspondence quality, and a unified timeline are not independently established by the included files. All three part packets now have open provenance/correspondence disputes and blocked analysis gates; their TIR hypotheses and observations are explicitly marked as unverified legacy claims, with unsupported personal names removed. The merged packet now has three open disputes and a BLOCKED merge gate; its four global IDs remain uncommitted candidates, not verified identities. The earlier abbreviated packet-scoped references in merged OI/TIR have been corrected to the full IDs in the part inventories. Unsupported personal names were removed from the part TIR observations because they are not established by retained visual evidence; the legacy source title is retained only as an unverified source label. The storage arithmetic was corrected (10 Mbps = 108 GB/day; 5 Mbps = 54 GB/day per camera, decimal units); these are illustrative calculations, not measured deployment rates. The claimed 10,000x reduction is not an evidence-equivalent comparison because the packet omits the source media and evidence needed to audit it.

## How to use this example

Use it only to discuss the *shape* of a multi-part CCTV investigation and the distinction between local packet identities and cross-part correspondence. Do not use it as an evidentiary account of the named event, a validated identity merge, or a storage benchmark.

Before promoting it to a teaching example, supply traceable source provenance, source-aligned evidence snapshots/manifests, reproducible part boundaries, and a reviewable correspondence record.

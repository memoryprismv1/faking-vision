## Evidence handling

Observed facts, relational conclusions, hypotheses and unresolved issues must remain distinct. Lack of a visible event in sampled frames does not establish that it never occurred outside the inspected interval. A generic vehicle appearance is insufficient to establish persistent identity across packets.
